About ICAR-IIWM

The ICAR-Indian Institute of Water Management (IIWM) was established on 12th May 1988 to develop improved water management technologies for sustainable agricultural production .

Mission: Basic, applied, and strategic research to address water management issues with institutional linkages, infrastructural support, and capacity building .

REWARD Project: World Bank funded initiative for watershed rejuvenation and agricultural resilience through innovative water management solutions .

Data Sources
Domestic Water Demand Data Sources (For Odagaon cluster)

Socio-economic survey of the SWS are based on both primary baseline survey and secondary sources of information such as block level of offices of Department of Agriculture and Veterinary . As per the census information and the primary survey, the total population of the Odogaon Cluster is 69,289 with male 36,449 and female 32,840 .

Per capita water demand is considered as 135 lpcd based on IS 1172:1993 .

Source:https://law.resource.org/pub/in/bis/S03/is.1172.1993.html

2. Livestock Water Demand Data Sources (For Odagaon cluster)

Livestock population are based on the 20th census of livestock carried out during 2019 . Daily consumption norms: Cattle/Buffalo (67 L/day), Sheep/Goat (7 L/day), Poultry (0.32 L/day), and Pigs (22 L/day) .

Source:https://epubs.icar.org.in/index.php/IJAnS/article/view/44302
